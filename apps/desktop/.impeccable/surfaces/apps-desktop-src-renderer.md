Scope: whole app (Electron renderer), visitor mode Operate. Seed 505a9fb8, assigned direction.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every application is a card in a catalog drawer. The app types the entries; the user reads the card and stamps it.
WORLD: library card catalog. Oak drawer front with a brass label holder as the titlebar; white index-card stock with pale blue rules and one red header rule; guide cards in manila, salmon, sky, mint and grey whose staggered tabs are the navigation and the status language; Courier Prime for anything the app typed, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything the user reads or does; violet date-due stamp ink for the one irreversible act.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the Missions drawer. A paste slot at the front, then run cards filed behind guide cards (Needs you, Running, Queued, Done), each card typed with company, role, portal and the last thing that happened.
SIGNATURE: a card files itself into the drawer when a job is added; the submit card receives a stamp.
REACH: onboarding fills the user's own catalog card by card (resume, cover letter, other documents, details, references); settings and profile are drawers of the same cards.
RISK: can tip into costume. Keep ornament to rules, tabs and stamp; content stays dense and legible.

Challengers declined: seedbed lobes, viewfinder HUD, emission-line rail, phosphor terminal, mesophotic dive, tensegrity column. Raise from the emission-line rail: state is never hue alone; every tab and chip carries its word.
