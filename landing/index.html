EVERY SURFACE OF THE APP FOLLOWS THE SAME QUIET, PAPER-AND-INK SYSTEM

It lives on your computer

Your whole life story, and no account to keep it in.

Most application tools ask you to upload everything first. This one is a desktop app. There is no sign-up, and no copy of your history sitting on someone else's disk.

SERVERS WE RUN
Zero

No account, no sync, no "anonymous telemetry." Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no us to upload to.

Keys in the OS keychain

Your API keys are encrypted with the operating system keychain before they touch the database. The worker gets them from a temp file that is deleted when the run ends. Never the environment, never a log.

Ordinary files you own

Resumes and letters are written as normal documents in a folder you pick, so you can open, edit or delete them without us. Nothing is locked in a format only this app can read.

Where traffic does go. Two places, both of your choosing: the model provider whose key you entered, and the job portal you are applying to. That is the whole list. Being straight with you about it matters more than a rounder number.

A

And it never fires without your go. The sensitive questions (veteran status, disability, anything about a past employer) are always flagged for you to answer yourself. No exceptions, no auto-submit, ever. Every run appends to a local history you own, so you can see exactly what the machine did and when.

Honest pricing

The app is free. You pay your own model bill.

There is no paid tier, because the work runs on your machine with your own API key. We have no server to bill you for.

Straight answers

The questions you are already asking.

Is it really all local?

The app, your files and your history are local. There is no account and no server of ours. To be precise about the parts that are not: your API keys are encrypted with the OS keychain, but your resumes and letters are saved as ordinary documents on your disk, not inside an encrypted container. And two kinds of traffic do go out, both to places you pick: the model calls you make with your own key, straight to your chosen provider, and the application portals themselves.

Does it apply to jobs without me?

Never. It prepares everything, the resume, the letter, every portal field, and then stops at the submit button. You review it all and click Submit yourself. Sensitive and EEO questions are always handed to you personally. The machine will not touch them.

Which portals does it understand?

The big ones companies actually use: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS and Taleo. It opens the real application page in a real browser and fills it the way you would, by reading the form, not guessing from a template.

Do I need my own API key?

Yes, you bring your own (Anthropic, OpenAI, and others). That is what keeps the app itself free and your data yours: the model runs on your account, not ours. Setup takes about a minute during onboarding.

Will it lie on my resume?

No. It reprioritizes and rewords your real experience to match the posting. It never invents jobs, skills or numbers, and gaps are declared honestly. Every resume is held to one page and checked against banned words and an em-dash gate before it is ever shown to you.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. macOS and Linux builds are on the way, since the app is built to be cross-platform from the start.
